## When You Need a Commercial License

The AGPL requires that anyone who runs a modified version of SpectraSherpa as a
network service must make the complete source code available to users of that
service. A commercial license removes this obligation and is appropriate when:

- **OEM / Embedding** — You distribute SpectraSherpa (or a derivative) as part
  of a proprietary product, instrument, or platform.
- **SaaS / Hosted** — You offer SpectraSherpa functionality through a hosted
  service and do not wish to publish your modifications.
- **Internal Deployment** — Your organization's compliance policy prohibits
  copyleft dependencies in production systems.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I evaluate SpectraSherpa under the AGPL before purchasing?**
A: Yes. The AGPL-licensed version is fully functional. Use it freely for
evaluation, research, and internal prototyping.

**Q: Does the commercial license cover SpectroChemPy?**
A: No. SpectroChemPy is a separate project licensed under CeCILL-B. The
commercial license covers SpectraSherpa only. If your deployment uses
SpectroChemPy, you must independently comply with its license terms.

**Q: Can I contribute back improvements under a commercial license?**
A: Contributions to the open source repository require signing the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). Your contributions will be available
under both the AGPL and commercial licenses.
